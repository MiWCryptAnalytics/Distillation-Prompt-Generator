# Interdisciplinary & Emerging Sciences — coverage map

These fields are, by design, cross-cutting: each fuses methods from several of
the other domains. The table therefore lists the nearest classification anchor
plus the domains each discipline *bridges* — the more useful framing here.
(Starts with `_` / not `.json`, so the engine ignores it.)

Reference systems used:
- **DDC** — Dewey Decimal: 001-006 knowledge/computing, 003 systems, 300.285
  computational social science, 500s cross-referenced
- **LCC** — Library of Congress: Q science (general), Q175.5 STS, Q295
  cybernetics/systems, QA76 computing
- **OECD** — Frascati flags these under "other" and interdisciplinary categories
  spanning natural, medical, social, and engineering sciences

## Notes & known gaps
- Each discipline here has "home" treatments elsewhere (e.g. Quantum Physics in
  Physical Sciences, Ecology in Life Sciences); the value of this domain is the
  *synthesis* — the concepts that only appear where the fields meet.
- _The audit round filled: computational linguistics/NLP, network neuroscience,
  synthetic biology, materials informatics, science of science, embodied AI,
  plus meta-science, forecasting, conservation science, ethnobiology, sensory
  science, and information design._
- _The gap-fill round filled: Color Science (colorimetry and appearance models,
  the synthesis between Optics' physics and the design fields' application),
  Game Studies & Ludology (theory side; player strategy craft stays in
  Practical's Competitive Games & Strategy), and Urban Science (scaling laws
  and network morphology, the quantitative synthesis neither Urban Studies nor
  Urban & Regional Planning holds)._
- Still open: behavioral data science (folds into Data Science + Computational
  Social Science for now).
