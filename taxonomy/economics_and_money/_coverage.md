## Boundary notes & known gaps
- **Academic economic theory & policy** — macroeconomics, microeconomics,
  development, econometrics — lives in Social Sciences; this domain is the
  money/markets/finance application.
- **Financial Economics** appears in Social Sciences as the academic field;
  here it is decomposed into markets, instruments, and institutions.
- _The audit round filled: VC & private equity, pensions, ESG finance,
  development finance & microfinance, Islamic finance, financial data science._
- _The gap-fill round filled all three open items: Sovereign Wealth & State
  Capital, Insurance-Linked Securities, and Household Finance (the academic
  counterpart of Practical's Personal Finance & Household Economics, per the
  theory/practice split)._
- _The ANZSRC/UNESCO sweep added Actuarial Science (reserving, graduation, and
  ruin theory — the credentialed quantitative core that Risk Management &
  Insurance's institutional concepts don't hold)._
- _The finance depth pass (JEL G-subcodes + CFA/CAIA/GARP bodies of knowledge)
  added 10 disciplines. Markets: Fixed Income & Credit Markets (duration,
  curve, and repo mechanics; securitization tranching stays in Real Estate &
  Housing Finance). Deals and issuers: Investment Banking & Capital Markets,
  Bankruptcy/Restructuring & Distressed Debt, Sovereign & Municipal Debt
  Markets, Credit Ratings & Analysis (rating-agency mechanics; retail credit
  scoring stays in Banking). Specialty lending: Trade & Commodity Finance,
  Project & Infrastructure Finance. Practice: Wealth Management & Financial
  Planning (advisor practice; behavior stays in Household Finance,
  sequence-of-returns stays in Investment & Portfolio Management), Corporate
  Treasury & Liquidity Management, and Clearing, Collateral & Securities
  Services (CCP waterfalls and collateral chains; settlement rails stay in
  Payments & Financial Infrastructure)._
- Judged covered, not split: asset pricing theory (Social Sciences' Financial
  Economics — the domain boundary), M&A advisory (Investment Banking +
  Corporate Finance & Valuation), fintech lending and BNPL (Banking + Payments
  + Financial Data Science).
- No open discipline gaps currently listed.
