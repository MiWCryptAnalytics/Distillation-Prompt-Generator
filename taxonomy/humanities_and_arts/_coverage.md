  P/PN literature & film

  literature, philosophy/ethics/religion, arts

## Boundary notes & known gaps
- **Philosophy of Science** sits here as a humanities/philosophy discipline; the
  *practice* of science lives across the science domains.
- **Logic** as a formal system is in Formal Sciences (Mathematical Logic);
  Argumentation & Informal Logic here covers reasoning in natural language.
- **Media & Communication** (empirical) is in Social Sciences; Film & Performance
  here is the aesthetic/theoretical treatment.
