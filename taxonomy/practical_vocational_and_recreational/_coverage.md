# Practical, Vocational & Recreational — coverage map

Breadth pass across hands-on, procedural knowledge — the trades, crafts, home
skills, and recreation — mapped to established classification systems. (Starts
with `_` / not `.json`, so the engine ignores it.)

Reference systems used:
- **DDC** — Dewey Decimal: 630 agriculture, 640 home & family management,
  641 food, 690 building, 745-749 crafts, 770 photography, 790 recreation
- **LCC** — Library of Congress: S agriculture, TH building construction,
  TR photography, TT handicrafts, TX home economics, GV recreation & sports
- **Vocational framing** — practitioner/operational knowledge, distinct from the
  engineering or physical-science theory of the same subject

## Boundary notes & known gaps
- This domain is intentionally the *practitioner's* operational knowledge.
  Several disciplines have theory-side counterparts elsewhere: HVAC & automotive
  *engineering* and materials are in Applied Sciences & Tech; personal finance's
  theory is in Economics & Money; photography-as-art is in Humanities & Arts.

  beekeeping & animal husbandry, sewing & tailoring, ceramics & pottery, music
  performance craft, bicycle & small-engine repair._
